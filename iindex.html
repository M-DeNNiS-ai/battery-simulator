Model: 1-RC Thevenin cell with chemistry OCV curve, temperature-dependent resistance, lumped thermal mass with cooling, BMS derating above 45 °C. Coulomb counting runs on a biased, noisy current sensor; the EKF fuses that current with voltage. Notice how the flat LFP and LTO curves starve the EKF of information, while NMC corrects quickly. Parameters are representative, not vendor data; interconnect, weld, AFE, MOSFET and runaway figures are simplified engineering estimates.
